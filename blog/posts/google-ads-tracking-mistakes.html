7 Google Ads Tracking Mistakes That Cause Bad Attribution

Review 7 Google Ads tracking errors, ValueTrack syntax mistakes, and auto-tagging conflicts that can affect GA4 reporting.

Linking Google Ads to GA4 does not replace a check of your landing URLs and tracking settings. If campaign details are missing or the channel looks wrong, review auto-tagging, ValueTrack and any redirects. Start with these seven common problems.

How Google Ads Connects to GA4

Google Ads communicates with GA4 through two mechanisms: auto-tagging (via the gclid parameter) and manual UTM parameters. When these two systems conflict, or when ValueTrack macros fail, attribution breaks.

Seven Google Ads Tracking Mistakes

1. Manual UTMs Without Auto-Tagging Override

If you populate manual UTMs and have auto-tagging enabled, GA4 prioritizes GCLID data unless you check "Allow manual tagging (UTM values) to override auto-tagging" in GA4 Admin. Without this alignment, reports can display conflicting campaign dimensions.

2. ValueTrack Curly Brace Syntax Errors

Google Ads ValueTrack parameters require precise casing and single curly braces. Writing {Keyword} (uppercase) or {{keyword}} (double braces) prevents token expansion.

3. Putting UTM Parameters in Final URL Instead of Suffix

Hardcoding UTMs in individual ad Final URLs forces Google Ads to re-review your ad whenever you update tracking parameters. Use the Final URL Suffix field at account or campaign level instead.

4. Third-Party Trackers Breaking Parallel Tracking

Using outdated click-redirect tracking templates that are incompatible with Google's mandatory Parallel Tracking can delay page loads or strip click IDs.

5. Redirects Stripping GCLID Query Strings

If an ad URL redirects and the redirect removes the gclid parameter, GA4 may lose some Google Ads campaign information. The session's final classification depends on other campaign, referrer, and advertising information available to Analytics. Check the redirect response and the destination page's analytics data.

6. Using utm_medium=google or search Instead of cpc

For manually tagged traffic, use a medium that matches the current Paid Search definition and check the source and other available traffic-source information. A value such as search may not match that definition by itself, but the final classification depends on the information GA4 receives.

7. Blind Spots in Performance Max Tracking

Performance Max runs across Search, YouTube, Display, and Discover simultaneously. Without dynamic placement parameters, you cannot audit where your spend converted.

An Example Google Ads Final URL Suffix

Paste this string into your Google Ads Account Settings under Tracking > Final URL Suffix:

utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={_campaign}&utm_content={creative}&utm_term={keyword}&matchtype={matchtype}&device={device}

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use GCLID, UTMs, or both in Google Ads?

Use both! GCLID passes rich Google Ads conversion data to GA4, while manual UTM parameters ensure third-party tools (like CRMs, Mixpanel, and data warehouses) capture campaign attribution cleanly.

Why does my Google Ads traffic show as direct in GA4?

This almost always indicates a 301/302 server redirect on your landing page that strips the gclid parameter, or missing GA4 tracking code on the landing page.
