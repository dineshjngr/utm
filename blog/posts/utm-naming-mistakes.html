7 UTM Naming Mistakes Marketing Teams Keep Making

Avoid the 7 most common UTM naming mistakes that fragment campaign reports, create duplicate rows in GA4, and derail marketing attribution.

Without strict governance, every team member invents their own naming logic. In as little as three months, GA4 reports accumulate dozens of variations for the exact same campaign, turning cross-channel analysis into a nightmare of manual spreadsheet cleanups.

Why Naming Taxonomy Breaks Down at Scale

As marketing teams expand to include contractors, performance agencies, and product marketers, link creation becomes decentralized. When five people create links for one campaign without standardized naming rules, GA4 records fragmented rows that make executive reporting impossible.

The 7 Critical UTM Naming Mistakes

1. Inconsistent Letter Casing Across Teammates

One media buyer uses Facebook, another writes facebook, and an agency writes FB. In GA4, these generate three separate lines in every dimension report. Standard: Force all lowercase, always.

2. Mixing Delimiters (Hyphens vs Underscores vs Spaces)

Hyphens (kebab-case) and underscores (snake_case) should never be interchanged casually. Choose one standard for word separation and enforce it universally. We recommend hyphens for campaign names and underscores for source and medium.

3. Unstandardized Date Formats

Using 05-12, May2026, and 2026_05 within the same analytics property ruins chronological sorting in GA4 and Looker Studio. Use the ISO standard: YYYYMM or YYYY-MM-DD.

4. Cryptic Internal Abbreviations

Tagging a campaign as utm_campaign=p1_us_t2_ret might make sense to the media buyer today, but six months later, neither the analytics lead nor leadership can decipher it. Use descriptive, self-explanatory names.

5. Stuffing Audience Details Into utm_source

Setting utm_source=facebook_retargeting_lookalike1 violates parameter scoping. The source is facebook; your audience targeting belongs in utm_content or utm_term.

6. Lack of a Centralized Taxonomy Source of Truth

If your team does not share a locked UTM dictionary or an automated tool like UTMCraft, naming divergence is guaranteed. Implement an approved list of sources and mediums.

7. Renaming Campaigns Mid-Flight

Changing your UTM campaign value halfway through a seasonal push splits performance data across two disconnected reporting entities in GA4. If you must adjust nomenclature, do so between campaigns, never mid-flight.

The Bulletproof 4-Part UTM Naming Formula

Adopt this battle-tested naming standard for utm_campaign:

[region]-[objective]-[theme]-[year]
Example: us-leadgen-whitepaper-2026

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use hyphens or underscores in UTM campaign names?

Hyphens (kebab-case) are widely preferred for campaign names because search engines and analytics tools parse hyphens as word separators cleanly without ambiguity.

How do I fix historical naming errors in GA4?

GA4 historical data cannot be rewritten. However, you can create Custom Channel Groups or custom calculated fields in Looker Studio with regular expressions to combine fragmented legacy names.
