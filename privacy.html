DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 • Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. Privacy by Architecture

UTMCraft builds and checks campaign URLs in your browser. We do not require an account or upload the campaign fields you enter to an application database. We use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits to this website, as described below.

All URL parsing, parameter concatenation, GA4 taxonomy checks, CSV exports, and QR code rendering execute exclusively on your computer or mobile device using modern Web Standards and Web APIs.

2. Campaign Data and Website Analytics

When using UTMCraft:

  • No Campaign Database: Campaign inputs are processed locally and are not stored in a UTMCraft account or application database.
  • Website Analytics: Google Analytics 4 measures page views and traffic sources across the website. Google may receive the page URL, referral, device and browser information, approximate location, and cookie or similar identifiers. Campaign parameters in the page URL may be included in this measurement.
  • No User Profiles: There are no user accounts, passwords, or cloud databases tracking your workflow.
  • No Retargeting Pixels: We do not embed Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or other separate advertising retargeting pixels.

3. Local Storage Transparency

UTMCraft uses the standard HTML5 localStorage API built into your browser to provide a seamless user experience. This data never leaves your device:

  • utmc_theme: Remembers your UI theme preference ("light" or "dark").
  • utmc_custom_presets: Stores any custom channel presets you create so you can reuse them across visits.
  • utmc_history_v2: Keeps a local record of URLs you generated or bookmarked, allowing you to search and export them to CSV.
  • utmc_destination_url: Remembers the most recently entered destination URL across pages and future visits on this browser.

These application settings stay in this browser. Google Analytics may separately use cookies or similar browser storage for traffic measurement. Clear the destination field to remove its saved URL, or clear your browser storage to remove saved settings and history.

4. URL Shortener Service Privacy

UTMCraft includes an optional URL shortening feature. When you explicitly click the "Shorten" button:

  • The final concatenated UTM URL is sent to a public URL redirection service (e.g. spoo.me, da.gd, or clck.ru) to retrieve an HTTP 301/302 short link.
  • The shortening provider receives the complete destination URL and ordinary network metadata such as your IP address. Do not place personal or confidential data in a URL.
  • To keep the destination URL out of shortening services, use the standard "Copy Campaign URL" button. The app’s normal page assets may still be requested from their configured hosts.

5. Compliance with Global Privacy Frameworks (GDPR, CCPA, CPRA)

UTMCraft minimizes application-level data collection, but legal compliance depends on your jurisdiction and how you use generated URLs:

  • Hosting: Our hosting provider may process standard request metadata, such as IP addresses and access logs, to deliver and secure the website.
  • Third Parties: Google Analytics processes website traffic data, Google Fonts is requested for typography, and optional shortening providers process URLs only when you choose the Shorten action.
  • Local Data: Clearing your browser storage removes locally saved settings and history. It does not erase analytics data already received by Google.

6. Security Architecture

UTMCraft is served over modern encrypted HTTPS with strict HTTP security headers, including Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: DENY, and strict Referrer-Policy.

7. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact our team directly at contact@utmcraft.com or visit our Contact Page.
