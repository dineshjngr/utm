2. Description of Service

UTMCraft is a free, privacy-focused, browser-based web application providing digital marketers and growth teams with tools to:

  • Construct valid, standard-compliant Urchin Tracking Module (UTM) campaign parameters.
  • Build multi-channel UTM matrix links in bulk for Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn, Email, and affiliate campaigns.
  • Inspect, decode, and audit existing campaign URLs for GA4 channel grouping compliance.
  • Generate vector and PNG QR codes for print and offline marketing collateral.
  • Generate shortened redirect links using integrated fallback services.
  • Store and export local campaign history exclusively on your device.

The core URL generation, inspection, validation, and QR rendering features run 100% in your local web browser using client-side JavaScript. No campaign URLs or parameters are uploaded to or stored on our servers.
