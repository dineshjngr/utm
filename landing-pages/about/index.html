About UTMCraft

UTMCraft is an independent project by Dinesh Jeengar. It provides browser-based tools for building and checking campaign URLs, along with guides about campaign tagging and analytics. UTMCraft is not affiliated with or officially endorsed by Google or the advertising platforms discussed on this site.

The Problem with Legacy Campaign Tracking

Teams may use different names, separators, and URL-tagging methods across campaigns. Those differences can make reports harder to compare, while a redirect may affect query parameters depending on its configuration.

Inconsistent tags and redirects that remove campaign parameters can make acquisition reports harder to interpret. Depending on the campaign, referrer, and other information GA4 receives, sessions may appear under unexpected source, medium, or channel values.

The 4 Principles Behind UTMCraft

1. Browser-Based URL Processing

URL creation, inspection, validation, and QR rendering run in your browser. UTMCraft also loads Google Analytics to measure site visits, and the optional URL shortener sends the destination URL to an external provider. See the Privacy Policy for details.

2. URL and UTM Checks

UTMCraft checks URL syntax, selected parameter fields, naming consistency, and a limited set of medium patterns. These checks can help flag common issues, but they do not reproduce every current GA4 channel rule or guarantee a live channel assignment.

3. Dynamic Ad Network Macros

Platform-specific builders include commonly used Google Ads ValueTrack, Meta Ads, and LinkedIn tracking tokens. Check each platform's current documentation for supported token syntax and account-specific behavior.

4. Tracking Guides

The guide library covers campaign naming, URL parameters, redirects, platform setup, and ways to investigate analytics reporting. Articles link to platform documentation where it supports the topic.
