Our Mission & Standards

Built for Privacy, Engineered for Clean Marketing Attribution

UTMCraft was founded on a simple realization: marketing measurement breaks because URL tagging is treated as an afterthought. We built a suite of client-side tools and deep-dive technical guides to help growth teams, PPC specialists, and RevOps engineers eliminate dirty data before spending a single dollar on ads.

The Problem with Legacy Campaign Tracking

Every marketing team starts with a Google Sheet. Within six months, multiple team members and agencies introduce casing inconsistencies (google vs Google), hyphen/underscore mismatches, non-standard channel mediums (paidsocial vs cpc), and broken query strings that drop parameters during 301 redirects.

The result in Google Analytics 4 is catastrophic: massive spikes in Direct traffic, swollen Unassigned channel buckets, and hundreds of millions in ad spend mapped to (not set) dimensions.

The 4 Principles Behind UTMCraft

1. 100% Client-Side Privacy

Your confidential campaign names, landing page URLs, target customer segments, and proprietary discount codes never touch our backend servers. All URL parsing, parameter validation, sanitization, and matrix exports execute directly inside your browser.

2. Built-in GA4 Rule Validation

Every parameter built or audited with UTMCraft is validated against official Google Analytics 4 Default Channel Grouping regular expressions. If your utm_medium will trigger "Unassigned", the builder warns you instantly and recommends the canonical fix.

3. Dynamic Ad Network Macros

We support native dynamic macro tokens for Google Ads ValueTrack ({keyword}, {matchtype}), Meta Ads dynamic URL parameters ({{campaign.name}}, {{adset.name}}), and LinkedIn Campaign Manager tracking tokens without breaking case rules.

4. Authoritative Knowledge Base

We don't publish generic marketing fluff. Our 35 deep-dive guides cover real architectural problems: redirect query retention, GCLID vs UTM hybrid tracking, email ESP link-wrapping mechanics, and multi-touch CRM hidden-field pipelines.
