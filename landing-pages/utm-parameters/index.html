What Are UTM Parameters?

UTM parameters (Urchin Tracking Modules) are query tags appended to destination URLs that pass campaign attribution data into analytics platforms. When a visitor clicks a tagged link, Google Analytics 4 reads these parameters and stores the marketing context as session-level and manual event-level dimensions - enabling precise attribution to the exact campaign, channel, and creative driving each conversion.

A fully tagged URL looks like this:

https://example.com/landing?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=spring_launch_2025&utm_id=cmp_98472&utm_content=search_ad_v1&utm_term=utm+builder+tool

GA4 supports 9 manual campaign parameters: six commonly used/core parameters plus three additional GA4 parameters. Some newer parameters currently have reporting limitations.

Google documents the traditional manual parameters plus utm_source_platform, utm_creative_format, and utm_marketing_tactic; Google also separately documents and recommends utm_id. Google notes that utm_creative_format and utm_marketing_tactic are not currently reported in GA4 properties.

🔎 How GA4 Reads UTM Parameters

GA4 reads UTM parameters on page load via the JavaScript SDK. They are stored as session-scoped and manual event-scoped dimensions in BigQuery export and campaign reports. They persist for the duration of the session (30 minutes of inactivity by default) and influence both Last Click and Data-Driven attribution models.

utm_source - Campaign Source Parameter

Definition: Identifies the specific referrer, publisher, platform, or origin that sent traffic to your website. It answers the question: "Where did this visitor come from?"

GA4 Dimension: Session source and First user source

utm_medium - Campaign Medium Parameter

Definition: Identifies the broad marketing channel or advertising method. It answers: "What type of marketing activity drove this traffic?" This is the most critical parameter for GA4 Default Channel Grouping - it is evaluated against regular expressions to classify sessions.

GA4 Dimension: Session medium and First user medium

⚠️ The Single Most Costly utm_medium Mistake

Using utm_medium=email as utm_source instead of utm_medium is the #1 error teams make. Source = where it came from (a specific newsletter name). Medium = what type of channel (email). They are different fields.

Core Parameters: utm_campaign, utm_id, utm_term & utm_content

utm_campaign - Campaign Name

Names the specific marketing initiative. Use descriptive, structured values: product_launch_q4_2025, summer_sale_us, brand_awareness_emea.

GA4 Dimension: Session campaign & First user campaign

Best Practices: Never use spaces. Use underscores or hyphens as word separators. Include quarter and year for temporal filtering: campaign_name_q4_2025.

utm_id - Campaign Identifier

A unique alphanumeric ID that matches the campaign record in your ad platform, CRM, or data warehouse. Separately documented and recommended by Google for GA4 Cost Data Import to tie advertising spend to conversion outcomes in GA4 Advertising reports.

GA4 Dimension: Campaign ID / Manual campaign ID

Example: utm_id=meta_c00298171 - where the value matches your Meta Ads campaign ID.

utm_term - Keyword or Audience Targeting

Primarily used for paid search to capture the keyword that triggered the ad. In Google Ads, use the {keyword} ValueTrack macro to auto-populate. In display or social, use it to identify the audience segment or targeting criterion.

GA4 Dimension: Session manual ad term

utm_content - Creative Variant or CTA Label

Differentiates between multiple creatives, ad copy versions, or calls-to-action within the same campaign. Ideal for A/B creative testing: hero_image_blue vs hero_image_green.

GA4 Dimension: Session manual ad content

Additional GA4 Parameters: utm_source_platform, utm_creative_format & utm_marketing_tactic

In addition to the six commonly used core parameters, Google Analytics 4 introduces three newer manual campaign parameters. However, marketers must be aware of their current reporting limitations:

utm_source_platform - Campaign Source Platform

Identifies the marketing platform that directed traffic to the website or app. While utm_source identifies the publisher or vendor (e.g. google), utm_source_platform identifies the sending platform interface (e.g. Google Ads, Search Ads 360, Display & Video 360, or Manual).

GA4 Dimension: Manual source platform

Example: utm_source_platform=Search+Ads+360

utm_creative_format - Campaign Creative Format

Identifies the creative asset material type used in the campaign, such as display banner, carousel, responsive search ad, or short-form video.

GA4 Dimension: Manual creative format

Example: utm_creative_format=video_vertical_9x16 or utm_creative_format=carousel

utm_marketing_tactic - Campaign Marketing Tactic

Identifies the high-level targeting strategy or marketing stage applied to the campaign, such as prospecting, brand awareness, onboarding, or dynamic remarketing.

GA4 Dimension: Manual marketing tactic

Example: utm_marketing_tactic=retargeting_cart_abandoners or utm_marketing_tactic=prospecting

⚠️ Google GA4 Reporting Limitation Notice

Google officially notes that utm_creative_format and utm_marketing_tactic are not currently reported in standard Google Analytics 4 properties. While GA4 captures these values on incoming events and passes them through to raw BigQuery event exports and custom explorations, they do not appear in pre-configured standard acquisition dashboard cards and do not influence Default Channel Grouping rules.

9 UTM Parameter Mistakes That Destroy Analytics

  1. Uppercase characters: Google and google create two different rows in GA4 reports.
  2. Spaces in parameter values: Spaces must be replaced with hyphens or underscores to avoid URL encoding issues.
  3. Tagging internal links: Avoid campaign UTMs between pages on your own domain. GA4 does not start a new session, but mid-session campaign values can distort event-scoped attribution. Use internal-promotion events instead.
  4. Missing utm_medium: Sessions without a recognized medium value fall into (Unassigned) in the Default Channel Grouping report.
  5. Inventing custom mediums: Channels like promo, organic_social, pdf, and blog_post do not match GA4's built-in regex rules and generate (Unassigned) rows.
  6. Using #hash anchors before UTM tags: Query parameters placed after a # hash fragment are never sent to the web server or analytics.
  7. Double-tagging redirected URLs: Adding UTMs to a URL that already has UTMs (e.g. via a redirect) creates duplicate or malformed parameter strings.
  8. URL-encoding the & delimiter: Some tools double-encode &amp; resulting in broken parameter strings.
  9. Inconsistent capitalization across the team: Without a shared taxonomy document, each team member invents their own casing and naming conventions, permanently fragmenting reports.

Frequently Asked Questions About UTM Parameters

How many UTM parameters does GA4 support and which have reporting limitations?

GA4 supports 9 manual campaign parameters: six commonly used/core parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_id, utm_term, utm_content) plus three additional GA4 parameters (utm_source_platform, utm_creative_format, utm_marketing_tactic). Google notes that utm_creative_format and utm_marketing_tactic are not currently reported in standard GA4 properties.

What does UTM stand for?

UTM stands for Urchin Tracking Module, named after Urchin Software, the web analytics company acquired by Google in 2005. Urchin's tracking technology became the foundation of Google Analytics.

Do UTM parameters affect SEO rankings?

No. UTM parameters carry no PageRank or ranking signals. However, if Google crawls and indexes UTM-parameterized URLs instead of clean canonical URLs, it may create duplicate content issues. Always set a canonical tag on landing pages and optionally configure Google Search Console's URL Parameters to ignore UTM variants.

Are UTM parameters visible to website visitors?

Yes. They appear in the browser's address bar after a visitor clicks a tagged link. This is normal. To hide them from end users in shared links, use the built-in URL shortener in UTMCraft, which creates a clean redirect with the full UTM string preserved behind the short link.

Can I use UTM parameters on non-Google Analytics platforms?

Yes. Many analytics platforms - including Adobe Analytics, Matomo, Heap, Amplitude, and Segment - support UTM parameter ingestion. While the specific dimension names may differ, the underlying URL structure is universal and platform-agnostic.

Does GA4 attribute conversions based on first click or last click UTMs?

By default, GA4 uses a Last Click model for standard reports. However, the Advertising reports use Data-Driven Attribution (DDA) when sufficient data exists, which distributes conversion credit proportionally across all touchpoints recorded during the conversion window.
