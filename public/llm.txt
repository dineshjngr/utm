# UTMCraft

> Free, browser-based tools and practical guides for building, auditing, and managing UTM campaign URLs for Google Analytics 4.

Canonical site: https://utmcraft.com/

## Tools

- [UTMCraft UTM Builder](https://utmcraft.com/): Build campaign URLs, inspect UTM parameters, create QR codes, and manage campaign links in the browser.
- [Bulk UTM Builder](https://utmcraft.com/bulk-utm-builder/): Generate campaign URL matrices and export them.
- [UTM Checker](https://utmcraft.com/utm-checker/): Inspect campaign URLs and check parameter values.
- [Campaign URL Builder](https://utmcraft.com/campaign-url-builder/): Build and review GA4 campaign URLs.
- [Google Ads UTM Builder](https://utmcraft.com/utm-builder/google-ads/)
- [Meta Ads UTM Builder](https://utmcraft.com/utm-builder/facebook/)
- [LinkedIn Ads UTM Builder](https://utmcraft.com/utm-builder/linkedin/)
