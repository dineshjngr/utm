💡 Why Universal Analytics Practices Break GA4

Universal Analytics (UA) was forgiving of mixed casing and non-standard mediums like medium=social-media or medium=email-blast. GA4 enforces rigid regular expression rules: if your medium does not strictly match its regex dictionary, your entire campaign session is assigned to (Unassigned), permanently breaking attribution models.

The 6 Core Google Analytics 4 Campaign Parameters

Google Analytics 4 reads query parameters appended to destination URLs to populate campaign reports, attribution paths, and conversion exploration funnels. Understanding each parameter ensures your data flows accurately into standard reports.

Parameter Required GA4 Dimension Example Value Primary Use Case
utm_source Yes Session source google, newsletter, meta Identifies the referrer or platform driving the user.
utm_medium Recommended Session medium cpc, email, paid_social Determines the marketing channel grouping in GA4.
utm_campaign Recommended Session campaign summer_sale_2025, q4_launch Names the specific product initiative, promotion, or theme.
utm_id Optional Campaign ID cid_984712, meta_c99 Used to match and upload offline cost data via GA4 Data Import.
utm_term Optional Session manual ad term enterprise+crm, b2b+software Tracks paid search keywords or specific audience segment targeting.
utm_content Optional Session manual ad content hero_btn_blue, carousel_v2 Differentiates creative variants, banner sizes, or copy variations.

How to Build & Deploy Campaign URLs: A 5-Step Process

  1. Identify the Clean Destination URL: Ensure your target page is live, resolves with an HTTPS protocol, and does not trigger an internal redirect that strips query strings.
  2. Apply Standardized Source & Medium: Always consult GA4 Default Channel Grouping rules. For paid social ads, use medium=paid_social; for Google Ads Search, use medium=cpc.
  3. Name Your Campaign with Strict Conventions: Use lowercase text and hyphens to separate words. Example: brand-awareness-na-2025 instead of Brand Awareness (NA) 2025.
  4. Differentiate Creatives with utm_content: If testing multiple images or ad copy angles in the same ad group, use utm_content=static_infographic vs utm_content=short_video.
  5. Verify in GA4 Realtime Reports: Before launching high-budget campaigns, click your tagged URL in an incognito window and check Google Analytics 4 → Reports → Realtime to ensure your source and medium appear correctly under Session Source / Medium.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Campaign URL Builder different from Google's official DevSite tool?

UTMCraft operates 100% client-side without transmitting your links to external servers. It adds live GA4 channel compliance scoring, instant link shortening, vector QR code export, bulk matrix generation, and automatic lowercase sanitization to prevent fractured analytics reporting.

Which parameters are mandatory in Google Analytics 4?

For accurate attribution, your Destination URL and utm_source are strictly mandatory. However, without utm_medium, GA4 cannot classify your session into a Default Channel Group, causing the traffic to default to (Unassigned).

Does utm_id replace utm_campaign?

No. utm_id complements utm_campaign by providing an alphanumeric identifier that matches campaign IDs across ad platforms (such as Google Ads or Meta Ads Manager). It is especially valuable when importing external cost or revenue data into GA4.

Why should I never use UTM parameters on internal website links?

Appending UTM tags to internal navigation (e.g. banners on your homepage linking to your product page) causes Google Analytics to end the current user session and start a new session. This destroys your original acquisition source, inflates your session counts, and causes artificial 100% bounce rates.

What happens if my destination page redirects (e.g. HTTP to HTTPS or non-www to www)?

Many web servers strip query parameters during 301 or 302 redirects unless specifically configured with a query string pass-through (QSA). Always ensure your destination URL matches the final canonical URL to prevent parameter loss.
